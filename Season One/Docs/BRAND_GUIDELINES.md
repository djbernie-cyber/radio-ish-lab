# RADIO-ISH LAB — BRAND GUIDELINES

## 1. Brand Name
- Canonical: **Radio-ish Lab** (capital R, hyphen, lowercase "ish", capital Lab).
- Short handles (fill platform usernames with this spelling): `@radioish.lab` or `@radioishlab`.
- Never "Radio ish", "Radioish Lab" in copy, or "RADIO-ISH" outside all-caps lockups.
- Tagline (pick one per asset, keep consistent):
  - "One house. Every sound."
  - "A DJ & Performing Artist Lab."
  - "Live from the Lab."

## 2. Voice
- Warm, editorial, night-out energy. Short sentences. Present tense.
- Descriptions steer with genre + feel + a concrete fact (length, format, "24-bit",
  "single continuous session").
- Never overclaim ("the best"); claim craft ("mastered to 320k, −14 LUFS standard").

## 3. Style Constants
- Artist names always rendered first, then fact stack. Example bio block:
  > **SDKZ** — Peak-time techno. Six-hour continuous session, 24-bit live capture.
  > Mastered for the club, delivered at 320 kbps.
- Genre descriptors (keep to these families): deep house · melodic techno ·
  experimental ambient · nostalgic/2004-era electronic · extended live techno ·
  peak-time techno · Ableton-recorded DJ set.
- Watermarks on video: bottom-right corner, small, `@radioish.lab`, white at ~60% opacity.
- Caption signatures: `🎛 live from the lab` / `#RadioishLab #RadioIsh`.

## 4. Hashtag System
- Brand: `#RadioishLab #RadioIshLab #LiveFromTheLab`
- Format/genre: `#DJSet #LiveSession #Techno #DeepHouse #MelodicTechno #Ambient #HouseMusic`
- Platform/behaviour: `#FYP #Foryou #Mixmag #ElectronicMusic #Edm #Underground #DJLife`
- TikTok/Reels captions: 3–8 hashtags max; Snapchat: 1–2 (via text sticker).

## 5. Audio/Visual IDs
- Every mix: ID3 artist = artist name, album = "Radio-ish Lab EPK", year = session year, genre per artist.
- Every upload filename: `{artist}_{YYYY-MM-DD}_{tag}.{ext}` (e.g. `sdkz_2026-09-03_master.mp3`).
- Thumbnail/cover: solid dark field (#0E0E10) + artist name centered white, drop shadow. 1080×1920 cover, 16:9 banner variant.

## 6. Do / Don't
- DO keep every deliverable in `epk/mix/` and social copies in `Social_Media/promo/`.
- DO lead clips with 0–2 seconds of movement before text appears.
- DON'T upload `.mov` sources to socials — convert to H.264 MP4 first.
- DON'T use unmastered stems as the audible "teaser" on socials — always use mastered mixes.
