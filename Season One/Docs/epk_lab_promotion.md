# Radio-ish Lab - Mixmag Style DJ & Performing Artist Lab

## Lab Overview

The Radio-ish Lab is a pioneering DJ and Performing Artist Lab promoted in the style of Mixmag, showcasing emerging and established talent across electronic music spectrum. Located at the intersection of audio-visual performance and DJ culture, the lab provides a platform for artists to develop their craft, record mixes, and connect with the global electronic music community.

## Technical Specifications

### Audio Format
- Mastered deliverables: MP3 320kbps (48kHz HQ master + 44.1kHz EPK master)
- Streaming/preview files: 64-192kbps where required by platform
- Lossless WAV/FLAC intermediates kept in `epk/{audio,processing}`

### Video Format
- QuickTime .mov files (HD upscaled where available)
- MP4 performance footage
- 9:16 vertical clips (1080x1920) for TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / Snapchat
- 16:9 banners and cover stills for streaming & press

### Social Platforms
- TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts / Snapchat: short-form vertical clips + captions
- Bandcamp / SoundCloud / Mixcloud: released mixes + original tracks
- All promotional assets standardized in `Social_Media/` with the `SOCIAL_PLAYBOOK.md`

### Release Strategy
- Weekly mix premieres on Mixmag.com
- Monthly artist features
- Quarterly compilation releases
- Artist mixer sessions and exclusive premieres
- Daily short-form drops on TikTok / Reels / Shorts / Snapchat from `Social_Media/promo/`

### Advertising
- Digital display ad lines (300x250, 728x90, 1080x1920) in `Advertising/`
- Artist one-pagers usable as sponsored posts, newsletter blocks, and program ads
- Campaign hooks and CTA copy ready for Meta, TikTok, Snap and streaming platforms
