# RADIO-ISH LAB — MEDIA KIT

**A DJ & Performing Artist Lab.** Eight artists, one continuous sound. Live-recorded,
Ableton-mastered DJ sets and performance sessions, production-grade and delivery-grade —
built to Mixmag editorial standard.

## Who We Are
The Radio-ish Lab is a DJ & Performing Artist Lab producing full-length, single-take live
sessions: deep house, melodic techno, experimental ambient, nostalgic 2004-era electronics,
extended live techno and peak-time club techno. Every set is recorded live, mastered to
320 kbps, QC-checked, and delivered with press-ready EPKs and short-form social assets.

## The Assets We Deliver
1. Full mixes — mastered 320 kbps, 48 kHz HQ + 44.1 kHz EPK master.
2. Original sources — 24-bit WAV / lossless FLAC intermediates.
3. Video — 1080p60 live footage + upscaled HD variants.
4. Short-form — 9:16 clips for TikTok / Reels / Shorts / Snapchat + cover art + captions.
5. EPK page per artist (`{Artist}/epk/index.md`) — link-out for press.
